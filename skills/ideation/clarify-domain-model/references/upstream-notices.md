# Sources and adaptation

Adapted from Matt Pocock [domain-modeling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d/skills/engineering/domain-modeling/SKILL.md) and its [glossary](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d/skills/engineering/domain-modeling/GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md) and [ADR](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d/skills/engineering/domain-modeling/ADR-FORMAT.md) formats.

Retained concrete domain scenarios, comparison with code, concise definitions, and sparse recording of consequential choices. Replaced automatic glossary creation and prescribed paths with existing canonical records. Context-qualified meanings may coexist; no forced global terminology or new ontology. Project additions include explicit decision status, history and affected references, partial outcomes, and a short human update. ECC sources in the broader proposal remain comparison candidates, not material incorporated into this adaptation.
