# Sources and adaptation

- [Matt Pocock research](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d/skills/engineering/research/SKILL.md), commit `4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d`. Retained primary-source investigation, claim citations, and one maintained research artifact. Removed mandatory background-agent execution.
- [ECC search-first](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC/blob/d2ef2680594f9a17526a6e1a2e18b3d242c5d17b/skills/search-first/SKILL.md), commit `d2ef2680594f9a17526a6e1a2e18b3d242c5d17b`. Retained need analysis, tool availability checks, existing-capability discovery, and fit assessment. Broadened package/tool searches to product and service integrations. Removed installation, final choice, mandatory fanout, and numerical scoring.

Added explicit evidence kinds, freshness/conflict handling, bounded research, an OKF-compatible brief template, and a short human update. No upstream scripts are bundled. These changes support the independently callable research step; they do not implement an ontology or attest real-world claims.
