# Sources and adaptation

- [ECC search-first](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC/blob/d2ef2680594f9a17526a6e1a2e18b3d242c5d17b/skills/search-first/SKILL.md), commit `d2ef2680594f9a17526a6e1a2e18b3d242c5d17b`. Adapted the adopt/extend/compose/build categories. Removed broad searching, installation, mandatory agents, and automatic execution; added eligibility, decision status, uncertainty, and a requirements handoff.
- [pstack laziness protocol](https://github.com/backnotprop/pstack/blob/4161d4ef5051574e825ffe70e2f43a2e49e4b3db/skills/principle-laziness-protocol/SKILL.md), commit `4161d4ef5051574e825ffe70e2f43a2e49e4b3db`. Adapted its minimal-change principle to the smallest sufficient product solution. The source is an engineering principle, not a complete selection methodology.

The decision brief, evidence distinctions, conditional outcomes, and concise human update are project-specific additions. No upstream executable helper is copied. New briefs use minimal OKF conventions; no semantic extension or validation runtime is bundled.
