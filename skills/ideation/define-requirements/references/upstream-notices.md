# Source and adaptation

[Matt Pocock to-spec](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d/skills/engineering/to-spec/SKILL.md), commit `4ae2317ddb22b046e26455e4f9c9b3aaaf62212d`.

Retained synthesis of known context, user-centered problem/solution framing, behavioral checks, and explicit exclusions.
