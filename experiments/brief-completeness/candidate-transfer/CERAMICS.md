# Ceramics implementation brief

## Intent and authority
Create a small Singapore weekend-ceramics site for finding an attendable class, comparing a few options and enquiring directly with the studio. It must feel calm and handmade; a booking platform is excluded. New explicit feedback prioritises beginner suitability, total cost and inclusions, prohibits recommending full or time-incompatible classes, and requires honest uncertainty about availability. Mechanics below are proposals, not user-approved designs. Provisionally serve adults on phones and desktop; do not assume child eligibility.

## Presentation and required facts
Use warm off-white space, clay accents, readable type and restrained imperfect details. Prefer generous practical cards to an image-heavy gallery. Permissioned workshop/process photos should provide texture without obscuring facts; no assets are supplied. A labelled layout study cannot establish finished handmade quality.

Cards prominently show dated session, Singapore start/end time, neighbourhood, technique, beginner suitability, total cost and key inclusions. Explain beginner suitability using evidence: experience required, prerequisites and instruction/support offered. Unknown suitability is “Beginner suitability unconfirmed,” never a beginner recommendation.

Details include exact address, duration, studio, accessibility, prerequisites, materials/tools, firing/glazing, take-home outcome, visit count, collection arrangements and mandatory extra costs. Distinguish included items, optional extras and unknowns. Total cost includes all known mandatory charges; when charges are missing, label the total unconfirmed and list known costs rather than displaying a misleading definitive price. Do not invent facts or accessibility assurances.

## Feasibility and discovery
Propose a compact weekend/date and available-time input, retaining choices across details and comparison. Order matching dated sessions by date. A recommendation must fit entirely within the visitor's available window and have evidence supporting availability. Full, cancelled, past or time-incompatible sessions are excluded from recommendations. If no time window is given, show browse options and invite time selection without asserting they fit.

Unconfirmed availability belongs in a clearly separated “Availability to confirm” group, not available/recommended results. It may offer an enquiry, never “Book” or a promise of a seat. Record source and checked date for session status; a past check cannot guarantee live availability. Unknown timing cannot pass the feasibility filter. Surface required additional visits and unresolved collection obligations before visitors choose; do not claim complete attendability when those remain uncertain.

Provide distinct loading, source-error and no-match states. No matches retains the visitor's constraints and offers explicit date/time edits; never silently relax them or suggest a known full class.

## Compare and enquire
Propose comparison of up to three workshops with aligned facts on desktop and repeated labelled sections on mobile. Preserve selections on returning from details; show cost, suitability and availability caveats in comparison too. Keep all controls keyboard accessible, visibly focused, high contrast and text-labelled.

Use a verified studio contact channel and editable enquiry identifying class/date. Opening a contact app does not send an enquiry or reserve a place. Provide another verified contact route on failure when available. No accounts, cart, payment or reservation infrastructure.

## Remaining decisions and checks
Single-studio versus directory, contact channels and data/freshness ownership remain unresolved; use neutral studio-labelled records provisionally. Verified sessions, costs, suitability, contact data and permissioned imagery are missing.

Check a phone shortlist and comparison for beginner clarity and complete costs. Walk a fitting class, full class, time mismatch, unknown availability, unknown cost and no-match case; inspect additional visits and enquiry class/date preservation. These are planned checks, not executed tests. Factual publication, rendered usability and user acceptance remain unverified.
