# Original requests

I want a coffee website that lets people discover specialty coffee from around the world. The main view should be a cartoonish map showing the notes and flavour profiles of coffee from each place, plus a short summary of the place and its coffee culture. Clicking a location should show cafes in Singapore that sell coffee from that place. Keep it clean and cream-esque. Use real cafes with sourced listings.

I want a small website for weekend ceramics workshops in Singapore. It should feel calm and handmade. Help visitors find a class they can actually attend, compare a few options, and enquire with the studio. Please do not turn it into a booking platform.
