# Coffee implementation brief

## Intent and authority
Create specialty-coffee discovery through a cartoonish world map, origin notes, flavour profiles and short cultural summaries that lead to real, sourced Singapore cafes. Clean, cream-esque styling and no checkout remain constraints. New explicit feedback requires one coherent map scene, meaningful photos, selection that focuses a place and visibly connects it to Singapore cafes, and origin comparison without losing context. The mechanics below are proposals, not user-approved designs. Provisionally serve curious Singapore coffee drinkers on phones and desktop.

## Composition and content
Use one consistent illustrated world scene: recognisable continental silhouettes, simplified boundaries, restrained cream ground, dark labels, warm accents and consistent texture/depth. Avoid assembling unrelated origin pictures into the map. Keep the overview visually dominant; use coordinated panels for origin details and cafe results.

Photos should contribute evidence and atmosphere in origin and cafe panels, with consistent crops and framing that belong to the scene. Propose origin/process photography and actual cafe photography, each accurately captioned and attributed. Never imply an unidentified photo depicts a particular origin or cafe. No photos or map assets are supplied: obtain original or permissioned assets, document rights, and inspect actual phone-scale composition before claiming visual completion. Missing photos require an honest fallback, not invented imagery or claims.

## Journey and interaction
Select an origin through labelled map targets or an equivalent keyboard-accessible list. Proposed selection gently pans/zooms to the origin and opens its notes, culture and cafe panel. Preserve world context through an overview/reset control and a persistent selected-origin label. Respect reduced motion; focus must not depend on animation, hover or precise tapping.

Make the relationship visible: use a consistent origin identifier across map selection, profile and matching cafe cards; propose a restrained route to a labelled Singapore destination with its connected result panel. Distinguish this visual relationship from claims of shipping routes. On phones keep overview/selector above details, with clear origin-to-Singapore labels rather than illegible long lines. Preserve selection, map view and results position after opening sources or details.

Propose comparing up to three origins via an explicit “Add to compare” control. Keep the current selection and map position while adding/removing origins. Compare labelled flavour notes, cultural context and evidenced cafe offerings side by side on desktop and in aligned labelled sections on mobile. Closing comparison restores the previous view; unsupported fields say unknown. Notes vary by bean, lot and processing, not guaranteed national flavours.

## Evidence and states
Each cafe needs name, Singapore location, specific origin relationship, supporting source and checked date. Label drinks versus retail beans, and historical versus currently verified offerings. Offer verified external cafe/contact links. Distinguish loading, source failure and no verified matches; missing evidence never means no cafes exist. Preserve comparison through recoverable errors and give routes to other evidenced origins.

## Remaining decisions and checks
Country-level exploration, both offering types and a small bounded origin set remain interim proposals. Owner must settle granularity, launch coverage and freshness ownership; researchers must verify profiles, culture and listings.

Before implementation acceptance, inspect one coherent origin-to-cafe composition with real permissioned photos; check readable geography, contrast and text selection cues. Walk selection, comparison, reset and source-return on touch and keyboard, including reduced motion, sparse coverage and failures. Verify each source supports the stated offering. Planning is ready; assets, factual publication, rendered quality and user acceptance remain unverified.
