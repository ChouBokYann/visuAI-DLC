## Display and crop rules

If using `object-fit: cover` or making a crop, append “cropped for display” to the credit; preserve enough context to identify the activity. Derivative images of the two BY-SA photos retain their respective share-alike licenses. Do not imply photographer endorsement. No photograph depicts the featured product, its named farmers, Nylon, PPP, or a Singapore cafe. Do not put these in a cafe-photo slot or label a pictured worker as an El Sendero producer.
