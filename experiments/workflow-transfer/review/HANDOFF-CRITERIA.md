# Fresh-reader review criteria

Prepared before producer delivery. Reviewers receive only their condition's HANDOFF.md starting path and may follow its declared references within that condition. They do not receive the other condition, evaluator, original conversation or this comparison's findings.

Within four minutes, establish whether a recipient can recover: (1) the original purpose and scope from a resolvable original brief, (2) authorization and known human owner without invented acceptance, (3) the current verdict and smallest next action, (4) runnable artifact and actual evidence paths, and (5) material unverified limits and dependencies. Report which facts were directly recoverable, inferred or missing; prose volume does not count as quality.

Run one representative safe synthetic preview/apply/undo journey using only the handoff's directions and references. Inspect actual names and contents. Do not repair the implementation or search outside the condition for missing information. Record command results and failures, including inability to execute the next action. A review may judge a handoff usable with gaps; it must not infer all product requirements pass from one journey.

Parent cross-checks claims against the frozen independent evaluator separately. Count consequential original-contract failures by scenario, unsupported broad completion claims by document, external correction requests per condition, and missing original-goal/source transfer. Report skips separately and retain the initial result even after correction. These are exploratory observations, not causal or statistical estimates.
