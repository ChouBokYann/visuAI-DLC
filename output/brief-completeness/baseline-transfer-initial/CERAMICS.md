# Weekend ceramics implementation brief

## Purpose and retained requirements
Help people in Singapore find suitable weekend ceramics workshops, compare options and enquire with a studio. Maintain a calm handmade appearance and an enquiry-only experience. No booking platform, accounts, cart, checkout, payment, seat holds or booking confirmation. No factual listings, photos or design system are supplied. This brief consolidates inherited context and new feedback; user acceptance and implementation authorization are not established.

## New explicit feedback
Beginners must understand suitability, total cost and inclusions. Do not recommend a full class or one outside the visitor's available time. State uncertain availability explicitly and never imply it can be booked.

## Proposed journey and eligibility behavior
Browse upcoming weekend classes ? enter available date/time and location preference ? inspect suitable options ? compare ? enquire with studio. Multiple studios remain a planning assumption, not a confirmed scope decision.

Collect the visitor's available start/end window in Singapore time. A recommended class must have a known complete session interval fitting within that window and verified non-full availability. Exclude full classes and time conflicts from recommendations. Until the visitor supplies a window, offer browsing without claiming time suitability. Classes with missing schedule or uncertain availability belong in a clearly labelled “Check with studio” group, separate from recommendations. A checked availability statement is not a seat guarantee; display its source and checked date, and require confirmation with the studio. How old evidence may be before becoming uncertain remains an owner/data-policy gap.

For beginner suitability, show prerequisites, whether beginners are explicitly welcomed, technique, experience/age restrictions and available instruction/support when verified. Do not infer beginner suitability from a class title. Unknown suitability reads “Beginner suitability not confirmed—ask studio.”

## Comparable information and enquiry
Use consistent field order: actual date/day, start/end time and timezone; studio and location; beginner suitability; total price and inclusions; technique/restrictions; availability and evidence. Itemize mandatory fees such as firing, materials or equipment where applicable. Distinguish optional extras. If any mandatory cost is unknown, label the total unconfirmed rather than advertising a misleading complete price. Explain later collection, additional visits and other commitments when applicable; never invent them.

Proposed comparison supports up to three classes, side by side on desktop and stacked with repeated labels on phones. Preserve selections through details/filter changes; visibly flag selected classes that no longer fit instead of recommending them. Announce comparison changes accessibly.

“Enquire with studio” identifies a verified destination; provide selected class name/date to copy. Missing contact details receive an explanation, not a broken button. Leaving for an external contact channel does not mean an enquiry was sent or a place reserved. The supported contact channel remains unresolved.

## Visual treatment, states and access
Use warm off-white, ink-like text, restrained clay accents, spacious aligned information and expressive headings with plain body text. Put schedule/cost before imagery. Keep texture away from essential details. Authorized photographs of hands, clay or studios may convey craft; never imply an unidentified image depicts a listed workshop. Inspect image rights and actual compositions before reuse.

Provide loading, retryable failure and no-match guidance that preserves filters. Use semantic headings, labelled controls, visible focus, non-colour statuses, readable contrast and mobile layouts without horizontal scrolling. Respect reduced motion.

## Completion and dependencies
Check beginner, confirmed/full/unknown availability, time-conflict and incomplete-cost cases through comparison and enquiry, including keyboard/mobile use. Verified source data, maintenance ownership, freshness policy and contact destinations block factual publication. Rendered usability, imagery and user acceptance remain unverified.
