## Alternatives, tools and risks

Reuse existing skills rather than introduce a quality framework package. A universal aesthetic rubric would conflict with diverse briefs; unlimited iterations would waste effort. Use local Markdown editing and before/after read-only scenario reviewers from the writing-skills process. No new runtime dependencies, paid services, model integration, downloads or third-party adapted text. Existing notices stay intact. Main risk: anchors becoming mandatory bureaucracy or rationalising incomplete essentials as “good enough”; tests must reject both.

## Acceptance

- Contextual anchors and capability assessment stay inside existing records, with no fixed quota or universal aesthetic.
- Real artifact inspection drives a specific specialist correction and accurate gap disclosure.
- Existing design-system label fixes avoid a new design process; dense operational UI is not forced into minimalism.
- Unavailable/unlicensed assets are disclosed, not replaced by a falsely finished approximation.
- Stagnant cosmetic polishing stops while keyboard/accessibility or other essential defects remain unresolved work.
- Changed paths and links are checked; before/after walkthroughs report actual findings without claiming proof of future visual quality.
