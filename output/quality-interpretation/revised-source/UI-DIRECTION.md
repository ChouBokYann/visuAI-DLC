**Newly revealed local preferences:** cream colors are acceptable; actual working-area views and daylight matter; an honest missing-photo state is preferable to repeated generic gradients; hours, power access and day price should appear together; avoid imposing photographic heroes throughout the site. These are specific to this synthetic workspace task, not universal product rules.

**Delegated choices:** use compact aligned fact rows and a responsive card stack; preserve restrained colors and clear hierarchy; place a concise missing-photo message where a working-area image would help.
