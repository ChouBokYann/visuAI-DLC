# UI direction — request A

Basis: [BRIEF-A](../BRIEF-A.md); inspected [HTML](../../../output/quality-interpretation/A.html), [desktop](../../../output/quality-interpretation/A-desktop.png) and [phone](../../../output/quality-interpretation/A-phone.png). Agent interpretation under delegated design judgment; no user acceptance or implementation authorization.

## Requirements and implications

**User requirements:** a calm, light website for finding an afternoon workspace; compare places, get a feel for each, avoid a wall of text, be useful and trustworthy; phone use matters.

**Necessary implications:** present comparable decision facts without making the user open every listing; distinguish known facts from unknowns; communicate meaningful place differences; keep the primary comparison readable within a phone viewport. The synthetic disclaimer cannot excuse unsupported venue claims.

**Delegated choices:** retain the restrained cream/green palette, readable dark text and simple card hierarchy. Use consistent short fact rows with price, hours, outlets and setting, rather than promotional taglines. Prefer stacked cards on phones; retain aligned columns on desktop. Reduce the oversized introductory space on phones so places arrive sooner. These are proposed treatments, not user-mandated tokens. Equivalent accessible arrangements remain open.

**Unknowns:** user budget/location/preferences; actual seat appearance, noise and daylight quality; currency identity; all real-world venue validity. No photos or licences exist in the supplied material. Do not invent ratings, availability, booking or travel information. These gaps do not block a truthful synthetic comparison.

## Defining qualities and observations

| Quality | Observation that would establish it |
|---|---|
| Calm and light, with useful density | Inspect desktop and phone compositions: clear heading-to-facts hierarchy, ample separation without burying places, restrained colors, short readable fact groups. A quieter visual tone must coexist with enough information to choose. |
| Comparable and trustworthy | Compare all three cards against the source notes: identical fact labels, visible prices/hours/outlet status, explicit unknowns, no unsupported superlatives. A reader can identify the cheaper option and practical limitations without opening a detail view. |
| A grounded feel for each place | Each listing communicates its actual known setting and uncertainty. Lantern: windows near entrance, not measured daylight; Yard: outdoor seating without shelter; Nook: setting unverified. Do not substitute repeated gradients for place evidence. |
| Comfortable phone use | Inspect at 390px and a narrower representative viewport: all card facts and actions fit, text remains readable, and comparison requires no page-wide sideways scrolling. Exercise keyboard focus and any real actions separately. |
